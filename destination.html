Overview

A coastline made for slow days

Cox's Bazar is home to 120 km of unbroken golden sand, the longest natural sea beach in the world. Mornings start with fishermen hauling in their nets, afternoons belong to the surf, and evenings turn the sky orange over the Bay of Bengal.

Beyond the main beach you'll find quiet coves, hill-top pagodas, tribal villages and fresh seafood grilled on the sand. With TravelOrio your hotel, transport and local guide are arranged before you arrive, so you can simply enjoy the coast.

  • Sunrise and sunset on Laboni Beach
  • Boat trip to Sonadia Island
  • Scenic drive along Marine Drive
  • Fresh seafood dinner by the sea
  • Himchari waterfall and national park
  • Buddhist temples and local markets

Top attractions

What you'll see

Laboni Beach

The main beach and the best place for swimming, parasailing and sunset photos.

Himchari

Cliffs, a small waterfall and a hilltop viewpoint over the sea.

Inani Beach

Coral-stone shoreline with calmer water and fewer crowds.

Sonadia Island

A quiet island reached by boat, known for birds and red crabs.

Plan ahead

Getting there and travel tips

How to get there

  • By air: about 1 hour from Dhaka, with several daily flights.
  • By bus: 9–11 hours overnight AC coach from Dhaka.
  • By train: Dhaka to Cox's Bazar by rail, about 8–9 hours.

Good to know

  • Book hotels early for Eid and winter weekends.
  • Swim only in flagged safe zones.
  • Carry cash for small shops and tuk-tuks.
  • Dress modestly when visiting temples and villages.
