Every trip is built on these, from the first message to the ride home.

01

Local Expert Guides

Our guides are born and raised in the regions you visit. They speak Bangla and English, know the quiet corners and the best light for photos, and stay with your group the whole way.

Good to know

Questions about trusting us

Are your guides licensed?

Yes. Our guides hold the local permits required for each area, and Sundarbans trips are accompanied by forest department rangers as the law requires.

What happens if bad weather cancels the trip?

Safety comes first. If a boat, ship or road is not safe, we reschedule your trip or refund what we have not already paid to hotels and operators.

Can I speak to someone before I pay?

Of course. Message or call us, ask anything, and take your time. Payment is only requested once you have confirmed your plan.

Do you handle group and family trips?

Yes. Groups of six or more get a lower per-person price and a dedicated trip manager. We also plan family trips with children and older travelers in mind.

Who is behind TravelOrio?

We are a small team based in Dhaka who love Bangladesh. Every itinerary is checked by someone who has been there.

Ready to travel with people who know the way?

Pick a plan and we will take care of the rest.
