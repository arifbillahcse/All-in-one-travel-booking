Quick answers

Before you write

How fast will you reply?

WhatsApp messages are usually answered within an hour during opening hours. Emails are answered within one working day.

Can I get a quote without paying anything?

Yes. Quotes are free, with no obligation. Payment is only requested once you confirm your plan.

Do you arrange trips for companies and large groups?

Yes. We plan corporate retreats, family reunions and school tours. Select "Group or corporate tour" in the form above.

Do you speak English as well as Bangla?

Our planners and guides speak both, and we can arrange guides in other languages on request.
